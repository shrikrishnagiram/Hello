The 2-minute guide

1The idea in one line

You buy two tickets: one that pays if the market shoots up, one that pays if it crashes. If a big move happens quickly, one ticket can become worth several times what you paid for both. If the market stays calm, both tickets slowly lose value.

⬆ Up ticketcalled a Call (CE). Pays if the market rises above its trigger level.
⬇ Down ticketcalled a Put (PE). Pays if the market falls below its trigger level.

2A ticket, with real numbers

Bank Nifty is at 60,800. An up ticket with trigger level 66,600 costs ₹140 per unit. Tickets come in packs (a "lot"), and a Bank Nifty pack has 30 units, so one pack costs ₹140 × 30 = ₹4,200.

On the end date, if Bank Nifty is at 68,000, the ticket is worth 68,000 − 66,600 = ₹1,400 per unit → ₹42,000. If Bank Nifty is below 66,600, it's worth ₹0 and you lose the ₹4,200. You can never lose more than you paid.

You don't have to wait for the end date. You can sell the ticket any day, and after a big jump its price rises well before then.

3The picture

This is what the two-ticket bet looks like. Across the bottom: where the market ends up. Up the side: your profit or loss. The flat red floor is the most you can lose. You only make money beyond the two "profit lines".

4The honest part: it usually loses

Tickets lose a little value every day the market doesn't move. Over many months the people selling tickets tend to win. Bought every month without thinking, this bet slowly loses money.

That's why this app only suggests buying about a week before a big news day (like an RBI interest-rate decision), when a big move is more likely, and selling soon after.

5Timing rules the app follows

✅ Buy in the window shown, about a week before the news.
💰 If the market jumps, sell half when your tickets are worth 3× what you paid, the rest at 5×.
⏱ If nothing happens, sell 2 days after the news. Ticket prices drop once the news is out.
🚫 Never hold into the last week before the end date. That's when tickets lose value fastest.

6Before you use real money

Only use money you are fine losing completely. That's the normal outcome of a single bet. Watch the app through one or two news days without buying first, to see how the prices behave. This app only shows information. It never places an order.
