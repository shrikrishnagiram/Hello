    Reality check: Bought mechanically every month, these strangles lose a little over time, because option sellers usually charge more volatility than the market delivers. They only make sense bought selectively, before a real catalyst while volatility is cheap, and exited into a fast move. Chances shown are what the market itself is pricing ("market-implied"). They are not a forecast or an edge. Move scenarios assume implied volatility stays the same. In a crash put volatility usually rises, so the put side may be worth more than shown.
